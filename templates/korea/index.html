Working in South Korea through the Employment Permit System

Most foreign factory, farm, fishing and construction workers come to Korea on the E-9 visa, arranged government to government through the Employment Permit System (EPS). After years of work, some move to the E-7-4 skilled worker visa and can stay long term. Figures as of {{ korea.as_of }}.

Avoid illegal brokers

  • E-9 recruitment runs through government agencies in each sending country. You don't need a private broker to get on the EPS roster.
  • Book the EPS-TOPIK only through your country's official EPS centre.
