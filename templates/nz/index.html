New Zealand: work visas and the route to residence

Most foreign workers come on an Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) tied to an approved employer. Residence then comes through the Skilled Migrant Category, which gained two new pathways for tradespeople and experienced workers in August 2026, or through a Green List job.
