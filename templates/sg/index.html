Singapore: work passes and the route to permanent residence

Singapore sorts foreign workers into three main passes by salary: the Employment Pass for professionals, the S Pass for mid-skilled staff and the Work Permit for most factory, construction and service jobs. Only EP and S Pass holders can apply for PR.
