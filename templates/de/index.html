Germany: work permits and the route to settlement

Graduates usually come on an EU Blue Card and can settle in under two years with good German. Without a degree, the main route is a paid vocational training (Ausbildung) or getting a trade qualification recognised, then working as a skilled worker. German is the deciding skill on almost every route.
